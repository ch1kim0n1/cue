# Cue

**Private AI overlay for meetings and code.**

Cue floats a glass panel over your desktop. Screen, mic, and meeting audio go to the AI provider you already pay for. Keys stay on your machine. No Cue account. No Cue server.

## Privacy in one line

Screenshots and audio leave your device only when you trigger a feature, and only to your chosen provider. See [privacy-policy.md](privacy-policy.md).

## System requirements

- Windows 10 2004+ (x64) or macOS 12+ (Intel / Apple Silicon)
- Microphone and screen-capture permission
- Your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or Nvidia API key
