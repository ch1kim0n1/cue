Privacy policy · Terms

Before you use Cue

Cue is a local overlay. It has no account and no Cue server. Screenshots and audio leave your machine only when you trigger a feature, and only to the AI provider whose key you pasted.

Cue tries to stay out of screen shares. That is best-effort, not guaranteed. Do not use Cue in proctored exams, interviews, or recorded meetings if that breaks the rules or local consent laws. You are responsible for how you use it, including consent when capturing other people.

Cue is for adults using their own notes, studying, accessibility, and practice.
